# Helix Logistics Platform — Service Level Agreement (v2.0)

## 1. Scope
Helix Logistics provides Fleet Tracking API and Dispatch Optimization services under the Standard Plan.

## 2. Uptime Commitment
Monthly Uptime Percentage commitment: 99.9% per service, measured per calendar month.

## 3. Service Credits
- Uptime below 99.9% but at or above 99.5%: 5% of the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 99.5% but at or above 99.0%: 15% of the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 99.0%: 30% of the monthly fee.
Credits must be requested by the Customer within 30 days of the end of the affected month; credits not requested are forfeited.

## 4. Support
Standard Plan support is business hours (Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00 local), with a 4-hour response target for P1 incidents. Extended 24x7 support is available for an additional $1,800 per month.

## 5. Billing
Invoices are issued on the 1st of the month for the prior month's usage. Disputes must be filed within 30 days of the invoice date.
