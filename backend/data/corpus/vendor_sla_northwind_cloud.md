# Northwind Cloud Services — Master Service Level Agreement (v3.2)

## 1. Scope
This SLA governs Northwind Cloud Services ("Northwind") delivery of Managed Kubernetes, Object Storage and Managed Database services to the Customer under the Enterprise Plan.

## 2. Uptime Commitment
Northwind commits to a Monthly Uptime Percentage of 99.95% for each covered service, measured per calendar month and excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance.

## 3. Service Credit Tiers
If the Monthly Uptime Percentage for a service falls below the commitment, the Customer is entitled to a Service Credit calculated as a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected service only:
- Uptime below 99.95% but at or above 99.9%: 10% of the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 99.9% but at or above 99.0%: 25% of the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 99.0%: 50% of the monthly fee.

## 4. Credit Application Rules
4.1 Service Credits are applied to the invoice for the month in which the failure occurred, before taxes.
4.2 Credits are calculated on the affected service's monthly fee, not on the invoice total.
4.3 Credits are rounded to the nearest whole US dollar.
4.4 The cumulative credit for any service in a month may not exceed 50% of that service's monthly fee.

## 5. Included Support
The Enterprise Plan includes Premium Support (24x7, 15-minute P1 response) at no additional charge. Premium Support may not be invoiced as a separate line item to Enterprise Plan customers.

## 6. Late Fees
Late fees of 1.5% per month apply only to undisputed amounts that remain unpaid 45 days after the invoice date.
