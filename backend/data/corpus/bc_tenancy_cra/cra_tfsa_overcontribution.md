# CRA — Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) Over-Contributions

## 1. What an excess amount is
An excess TFSA amount arises when the total contributions in a year exceed the holder's available TFSA contribution room. Withdrawals restore room only in the following calendar year, so withdrawing an excess amount does not remove the penalty for the months it was held.

## 2. Penalty rate
The Canada Revenue Agency charges a tax of 1% per month on the highest excess TFSA amount in the month, for each month that the excess stays in the account. The penalty continues until the excess is withdrawn or room becomes available.

## 3. Formula
Penalty = highest excess amount x 1% x number of months with the excess. For example, an excess of $4,500 held for 3 months is $4,500 x 1% x 3 = $135.00. An excess of $4,500 held for a single month is $45.00.

## 4. Filing
The holder files Form RC243 (TFSA Return) and pays the tax by 30 June of the following year. Interest may be charged on unpaid amounts. The CRA may waive the tax in limited cases where the excess was an honest mistake and was promptly withdrawn.

## 5. Practical guidance
Check the contribution room in My Account before depositing. Transfers between TFSA issuers do not count as contributions. A direct transfer should be requested so that it is not treated as a withdrawal and a fresh contribution.
