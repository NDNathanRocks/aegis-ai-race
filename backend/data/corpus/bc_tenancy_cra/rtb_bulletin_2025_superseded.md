# Historical / Superseded 2025 RTB Bulletin — Annual Rent Increase Limit

## 1. Status
This bulletin applied to rent increases that took effect during calendar year 2025 only. It is superseded by the 2026 allowable increase and must not be used for increases taking effect in 2026 or later. It is kept for history.

## 2. The 2025 figure
For 2025 the allowable annual rent increase was 3.0%. At $2,400 per month the 2025 maximum was $72.00, giving $2,472.00. This is not the 2026 rule. The 2026 allowable increase is lower (2.3%).

## 3. Why it still appears online
Older web pages, landlord forms and forum answers still quote 3.0%. A landlord who relies on the 2025 percentage for a 2026 increase is applying the wrong year's cap.
