# Ontario Landlord and Tenant Board — Comparison Notes (not applicable in BC)

## 1. Why this document exists
Ontario rules are often quoted in online answers about Canadian rent and are easily confused with British Columbia rules. Ontario law does not apply to a rental unit in New Westminster or any other BC city.

## 2. Ontario rent increase guideline
Ontario publishes an annual rent increase guideline set by the province, which was 2.5% for 2025. The increase is given on Form N1 with 90 days' notice. Ontario rent control does not apply to units first occupied after November 15, 2018.

## 3. Ontario landlord's own use
Ontario uses Form N12 for landlord's own use, with at least 60 days' notice and a termination date at the end of a rental period. Ontario permits the landlord's child, parent, spouse or caregiver, and the equivalent family of the spouse. These rules do not make the BC four-month notice shorter.

## 4. Why the difference matters
A tenant in BC cannot rely on Form N1 or Form N12 and a BC landlord cannot rely on the Ontario guideline. For BC use RTB-7 for a rent increase and RTB-32 for landlord's use.
