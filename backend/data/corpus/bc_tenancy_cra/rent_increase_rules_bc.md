# BC Residential Tenancy Act — Rent Increase Rules (current, 2026)

## 1. Maximum annual rent increase
In British Columbia a landlord may raise rent only once in any 12-month period, and only by no more than the annual allowable percentage set by regulation. The allowable increase for 2026 is 2.3%. The cap for 2027 has been published as 2.2%. The cap is a ceiling on the percentage increase of the current rent, not a target and not a starting point for negotiation.

## 2. How to compute the allowable increase
Maximum increase in dollars = current monthly rent x 2.3%. Maximum new rent = current rent + that amount. For example, at $2,400 per month the maximum increase is $55.20 and the maximum new rent is $2,455.20. An increase of 6% on $2,400 is $144.00 (new rent $2,544), which is more than the cap and is not allowed.

## 3. Notice requirement
The landlord must give the tenant at least 3 full months' written notice on the approved form, the Notice of Rent Increase (RTB-7). The three months are counted in full months: notice given by email "yesterday" for "next month" is too short. An email or verbal message alone is not a valid rent increase notice.

## 4. Effect of an unlawful increase
A notice that asks for more than the cap, or that is not on the correct form, or that does not give three full months, is not valid for the excess. The tenant continues to pay the current rent, or the lawful maximum if the notice is otherwise valid, and may dispute the notice with the Residential Tenancy Branch. A landlord cannot threaten eviction to force payment of an unlawful increase.

## 5. Exceptions
A landlord may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an increase above the cap only for specified extraordinary reasons, such as significant repairs or a significant increase in operating expenses. Application and a decision come before any such increase takes effect.

## 6. Tenant rights summary
Pay the lawful amount. Keep copies of all notices. If the landlord disagrees, the dispute resolution process of the Residential Tenancy Branch decides.
