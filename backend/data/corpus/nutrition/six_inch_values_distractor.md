# Six-Inch Values (NOT for footlong orders)

## 1. Warning
The numbers in this document are for a SIX-INCH sandwich only. They are roughly half of the footlong values. Do not use them to total a footlong order. They are kept because older menu cards still show them.

## 2. Six-inch values (one standard portion)
| Item | kcal | protein g | sodium mg |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Italian Herbs & Cheese bread (6-inch) | 220 | 9 | 390 |
| Rotisserie-Style Chicken (6-inch) | 100 | 19 | 260 |
| Habanero Jack cheese (6-inch) | 50 | 3 | 120 |
| Nashville-Style Hot Sauce (6-inch) | 40 | 0 | 190 |
| Chipotle Southwest sauce (6-inch) | 45 | 0 | 130 |
| crispy onions (6-inch) | 25 | 0 | 25 |
| jalapenos (6-inch) | 5 | 0 | 60 |
| black olives (6-inch) | 15 | 0 | 45 |

## 3. Illustration of the trap
Adding the six-inch values above (with a single cheese portion) gives only about 500 kcal and 1,220 mg sodium, which would wrongly look compliant for the calorie and sodium caps. A footlong order must use the footlong matrix.
