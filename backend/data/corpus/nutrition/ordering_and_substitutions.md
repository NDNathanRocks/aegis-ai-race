# Ordering Rules, Substitutions and Special Diets (demo)

## 1. Building an order
A custom order is placed ingredient by ingredient: one bread, one protein, optional cheese, optional sauces and any number of toppings. A "double" request applies to a protein or a cheese and is charged as an extra portion. Sauces and toppings are always one standard portion in the demo unless the customer asks for a light amount, which is not modelled by the audit.

## 2. Substitutions
A customer may swap any ingredient for another in the same category at no charge. The audit always uses the final ingredient list. When a customer asks for a dairy-free version, staff should remove cheese, choose a bread without milk and choose sauces whose allergen column does not list milk, then confirm with the customer.

## 3. Special diets
- Low-calorie orders usually pick a lean protein, no cheese and a light sauce.
- High-protein orders pick a large protein portion and may double the protein.
- Low-sodium orders avoid cured meats, pickled toppings and salty cheeses.
- Allergen-sensitive orders must be flagged at the counter so gloves can be changed and surfaces wiped.

## 4. Order confirmation
The order screen shows the ingredient list and the allergen summary. The customer confirms before payment. If an allergen is present in the final list, the screen shows a warning with the allergen name.

## 5. Complaints and corrections
If a customer receives an ingredient they did not order, the restaurant replaces the sandwich. A complaint about an allergen exposure is escalated to the manager immediately and logged.

## 6. Data freshness
The ingredient matrix is reviewed each quarter. Numbers in this demo are illustrative and are never presented as an official restaurant dataset.
