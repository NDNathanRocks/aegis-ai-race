# Air Canada — Aeroplan Status Tiers, Lounges and Upgrades (demo summary)

## 1. Status tiers
Aeroplan status is earned in a calendar year from status qualifying points and flights. The tiers are 25K, 35K, 50K, 75K and Super Elite. Higher tiers receive priority check-in, priority boarding and priority baggage handling. Status is valid until the end of the following calendar year.

## 2. Lounge access
Maple Leaf Lounge access applies to customers travelling in international or transborder business class, and to Super Elite members and Star Alliance Gold members travelling on a Star Alliance flight, with one guest in some cases. Access is for the day of departure and cannot be used on arrival except at designated airports. Entry requires a boarding pass for a flight departing the same day.

## 3. Upgrades
Complimentary upgrades are offered on a space-available basis and are processed in order of status tier, fare class and the time of the request. Upgrade eligibility depends on the fare brand. The cheapest basic fares are not eligible. A confirmed upgrade is shown on the boarding pass and may be rescinded only for an aircraft change.

## 4. Seat selection
Standard seat selection is charged on most economy fares unless the member holds status. Preferred seats near the front and extra-legroom seats carry a higher fee. Seat selection fees are refundable only if the airline changes the aircraft and the seat is no longer available.

## 5. Points redemption
Points can be redeemed for flights, seat selection and upgrades. Taxes and carrier-imposed charges are payable in cash on reward flights. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

## 6. Name changes and ticket changes
A name correction is free for minor spelling errors. Other changes depend on the fare brand and are charged a change fee, or are free on flexible fares.
