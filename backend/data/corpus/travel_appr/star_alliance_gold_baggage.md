# Air Canada — Star Alliance Gold Baggage Allowance (demo summary)

## 1. Eligibility
Passengers who hold Star Alliance Gold status through a participating frequent flyer programme, including Aeroplan Super Elite 50K and above, receive status benefits on Air Canada flights when the status number is on the booking.

## 2. Free checked baggage for Star Alliance Gold
- International itineraries: two free checked bags, each up to 32 kg (70 lb), in addition to the standard fare allowance.
- Domestic Canada itineraries: one extra free checked bag compared with the fare allowance.
Gold status does not raise the 23 kg Economy bag weight limit unless the status allowance above states 32 kg.

## 3. Cabin baggage
Star Alliance Gold members may bring one personal item and one carry-on item, with priority tag handling at check-in.

## 4. Oversize and overweight
Bags above 32 kg and bags over the 158 cm linear size limit are charged oversize and overweight fees, regardless of status.

## 5. Interaction with the baggage fee refund
Free bags do not generate a baggage fee refund because no fee was paid. Only bags with a paid checked baggage fee are refundable when delayed.
