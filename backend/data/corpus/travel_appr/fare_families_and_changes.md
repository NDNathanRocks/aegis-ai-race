# Air Canada — Fare Families and Ticket Changes (demo summary)

## 1. Fare families
Economy fares are sold in families that decide which extras are included. The cheapest family has no seat selection, no free changes and earns fewer points. Standard and Flex families add a free carry-on, seat selection and the ability to change or cancel. Premium economy and business families add priority services and larger baggage allowances.

## 2. Voluntary changes
A customer who changes a ticket by choice pays any fare difference plus the change fee set by the fare family. Changes made at least 24 hours after booking and before departure are allowed on all but the most restrictive fares. A change on the day of travel to an earlier flight may be offered at a reduced fee, subject to space.

## 3. Cancellation and refunds
A ticket bought directly from the airline can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of booking when the flight leaves at least seven days later. After that, the refund depends on the fare family. Non-refundable fares keep the taxes refundable, and the unused value may be held as a credit for use within one year.

## 4. Name and date corrections
A small name correction, such as a spelling error of up to three letters, is free. A change of traveller is not allowed. A date change is treated as a voluntary change.

## 5. Check-in and boarding
Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before a domestic departure. Boarding closes 15 minutes before departure and a customer who arrives after boarding closes may be rebooked on the next available flight at the airline's discretion.

## 6. Connections
The minimum connection time depends on the airport and on whether the itinerary is domestic, transborder or international. A protected connection means the airline rebooks the customer if an earlier segment is late.
