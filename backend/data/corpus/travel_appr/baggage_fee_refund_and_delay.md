# Air Canada — Checked Baggage Delay and Fee Refund Policy (demo summary)

## 1. Scope
This document summarises the demo rule for checked baggage on Canadian domestic and transborder itineraries. Baggage fee amounts follow the first and second checked bag fees in the carrier's tariff. It is a simplified summary prepared for this demo.

## 2. Refund of the checked baggage fee
If a checked bag is delivered to the passenger more than 24 hours after the passenger's own arrival at the final destination, the airline must refund the checked baggage fee paid for that bag. The refund is per bag and equals the fee actually paid. Two delayed bags that each cost CAD $35 produce a refund of CAD $70.

## 3. Refund is separate from delay compensation
The baggage fee refund is owed in addition to any flight-delay compensation under the Air Passenger Protection Regulations. The two amounts are added together; the refund is not deducted from, or capped by, the delay compensation.

## 4. Reasonable expenses
While a bag is delayed the passenger may also claim reasonable, receipted expenses for essentials such as toiletries and a change of clothes. The demo rule has no fixed daily amount; the airline decides what is reasonable and must reply in writing.

## 5. Claim time limit
A delayed baggage claim must be reported to the airline in writing within 21 days of the date the bag was received. Keep the baggage tag, the property irregularity report and receipts.

## 6. Example
A passenger flies Vancouver to Toronto, pays CAD $35 for each of two checked bags, and both bags arrive 30 hours after the passenger. Each bag arrives more than 24 hours late, so both fees are refundable: 2 x $35 = CAD $70.
