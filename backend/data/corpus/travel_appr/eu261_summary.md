# EU Regulation EC 261/2004 (EU261) — Summary (conflicting regime, not applicable to Canadian domestic flights)

## 1. Scope of EU261
EU261 is a European Union regulation. It applies to flights departing from an airport in the EU, and to flights arriving in the EU that are operated by an EU carrier. It does not cover a flight between two Canadian cities such as Vancouver to Toronto on a Canadian carrier. A gate agent who mentions EU261 for a Canadian domestic flight is quoting the wrong legal regime.

## 2. EU261 compensation bands (in-scope flights only)
- Distance up to 1,500 km: EUR 250.
- Distance 1,500 to 3,500 km, or any intra-EU flight over 1,500 km: EUR 400.
- Distance over 3,500 km, extra-EU: EUR 600.
Amounts are paid in euros, per passenger, for arrival delays of three hours or more or for cancellation, unless extraordinary circumstances apply.

## 3. Why this matters
The EUR 600 figure often appears in online discussions about flight delay compensation. It is not the entitlement for a Canadian domestic APPR claim. For a Canadian domestic delay on a large carrier the APPR bands in CAD apply.

## 4. Choosing one regime
A passenger cannot stack EU261 and APPR for the same disruption. For a flight outside EU261's scope, only the APPR schedule applies.
