# Air Canada Delay Goodwill Voucher Promotion (2019) — EXPIRED, DO NOT USE

## 1. Status
This promotion ended on 31 December 2019 and is retained only as history. It does not describe any current entitlement.

## 2. What the promotion offered
During 2019 the airline's customer relations team could offer a travel voucher worth CAD $300 to passengers delayed more than four hours. The voucher was discretionary, expired after 12 months, and could be used only on future Air Canada bookings.

## 3. Why it no longer applies
The Air Passenger Protection Regulations replaced goodwill vouchers with mandatory minimum compensation. A $300 voucher is not a lawful substitute for the APPR cash compensation, which is higher for a delay of six hours or more. Agents who still offer the old $300 voucher are quoting an expired promotion.
