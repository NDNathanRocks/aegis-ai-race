# Northwind Cloud Services — Legacy Master SLA (v2.4, 2023) — SUPERSEDED

## 1. Status of this document
This is the 2023 edition of the Northwind Master SLA. It was superseded by Master SLA v3.2 and applies only to billing months before January 2024. It is retained for audit history. Do not use it to price or audit any current invoice.

## 2. Legacy Uptime Commitment
Northwind committed to 99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage under the 2023 terms, measured per calendar month.

## 3. Legacy Service Credit Tiers (superseded)
- Uptime below 99.9% but at or above 99.0%: 10% credit on the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 99.0%: 25% credit on the monthly fee.
These bands were replaced in v3.2. A 99.62% month is no longer a 10% credit event.

## 4. Legacy Support Terms (superseded)
Under the 2023 Enterprise Plan, 24x7 Premium Support was an optional add-on billed at $2,400 per month. From v3.2, Premium Support is included in the Enterprise Plan at no additional charge and may not be invoiced separately.

## 5. Legacy Rounding
Credits were rounded up to the next $100 under the 2023 terms. Current terms round to the nearest whole US dollar.
