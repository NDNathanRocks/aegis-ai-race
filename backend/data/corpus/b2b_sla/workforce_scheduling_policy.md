# Workforce Scheduling Policy (v2.7)

## 1. Shift Rules
1.1 Standard shifts are 8 hours with a 30-minute unpaid meal break after the fifth hour.
1.2 Employees must have at least 11 hours of rest between shifts.
1.3 No employee may be scheduled more than 6 consecutive days.

## 2. On-Call
2.1 On-call rotations are one week long, handing over Monday 09:00 local time.
2.2 On-call engineers receive a stipend of $350 per week plus 1.5x pay for time spent working an incident outside normal hours.
2.3 No engineer may be on call for more than 2 consecutive weeks.

## 3. Schedule Publication
Schedules are published 14 days in advance. Changes inside 7 days require manager approval and employee consent, except during P1 incidents.

## 4. Overtime
Hours beyond 40 per week are paid at 1.5x. Overtime above 10 hours per week requires director approval.
